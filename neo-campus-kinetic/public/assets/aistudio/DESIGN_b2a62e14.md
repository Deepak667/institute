## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a punchy, hyper-focused tech campus atmosphere. It bridges the discipline of technical career acceleration with an electric, youthful energy. Designed for modern tech academies, coding bootcamps, and career transition platforms, the UI commands attention while remaining highly legible, intuitive, and welcoming to learners of all levels.

The visual style blends modern neo-brutalism with polished interactive SaaS components. It leans away from sterile corporate platforms and dry academic portals, opting instead for high-contrast structural framing, prominent stroke weights, expansive border radii, and energetic micro-interactions. The interface evokes forward momentum, technical confidence, and educational rigor.

## Colors

The palette establishes an electric visual rhythm grounded in deep slate structure:

- **Primary (`#06B6D4`)**: Energetic cyan acts as the core catalyst, used for primary action states, focal interactive elements, and progressive learning highlights.
- **Secondary (`#4F46E5`)**: Electric indigo introduces technical authority, used for deep navigational anchors, structural card headers, and complementary gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Fresh emerald functions as a positive reinforcement metric, signaling certification unlocks, enrollment validation, and student placement success.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Deep slate navy replaces flat black, serving as the architectural backbone for typography, hard structural borders, high-contrast badges, and grounding backdrops.
- **Surface & Backgrounds**: The primary canvas defaults to `#F8FAFC`, paired with crisp `#FFFFFF` card surfaces to let neo-brutalist structural outlines and saturated cyan accents snap forward cleanly without chromatic vibration.

## Typography

The type system pairs the punchy, experimental personality of **Bricolage Grotesque** with the immaculate technical clarity of **Hanken Grotesk**.

- **Headlines & Display**: Rendered in Bricolage Grotesque with snug negative tracking. Its quirky, exaggerated ink traps and energetic curves deliver the tech-forward campus personality without sacrificing legibility.
- **Body & Technical Data**: Set in Hanken Grotesk, offering an open x-height, neutral tone, and effortless readability across dense curriculum outlines, code-adjacent modules, and step-by-step progress tracking.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Set in medium to bold weights of Hanken Grotesk with subtle positive tracking to maintain crisp scanning across badges, pill tags, and status ribbons.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate dense programmatic data, modular curriculum blocks, and dynamic promotional banners:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` screen margins. Elements align cleanly into dual, triple, or asymmetrical 4/8 column layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Multi-column cards stack vertically into linear step progressions.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Governed by multiples of 4px/8px, using `space-md` for standard internal component padding and `space-xl` to `space-2xl` for sweeping campus landing sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through neo-brutalist tactile elevation rather than soft diffused blurs. The system relies on hard structural offsets, distinct outlines, and flat surface layers:

- **Hard-Edge Drops**: Interactive cards and hero buttons utilize solid, unblurred 2px to 4px offset box-shadows cast in deep slate navy (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 1)` or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)` for passive layers).
- **Structural Outlines**: All interactive containers, cards, and input panels feature explicit `2px` borders in neutral slate (`#0F172A`) or subtle stroke tints (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Tactile State Press**: On `:hover`, elements shift outward via translation (`translate(-2px, -2px)`) while expanding the hard drop-shadow; on `:active`, elements translate down flush (`translate(0, 0)`) with zero shadow offset, creating a physical tactile click.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** roundedness strategy across all components. This high-radius treatment balances the sharp neo-brutalist 2px outlines by introducing organic, student-friendly contours:

- **Pill Elements**: Buttons, status badges, chips, and search inputs utilize full pill radii (`9999px` / rounded-full).
- **Cards & Surfaces**: Major surface containers, modal views, and syllabus cards utilize large rounded corners (`2rem` to `3rem`), generating comfortable frame contours.
- **Embedded Media**: Previews, terminal code blocks, and instructor avatars follow the same generous corner rounding, maintaining a unified visual language across all viewport densities.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Filled with vibrant cyan (`#06B6D4`), dark slate text (`#0F172A`), `2px` solid slate border, full pill radius, and a hard 3px bottom-right drop shadow. Translates flush on click.
  - *Secondary*: Electric indigo fill (`#4F46E5`) with crisp white text, or pure white surface with slate border and energetic cyan hover accent.
- **Chips & Badges**:
  - Full pill silhouette with compact label typography (`label-sm`).
  - Course status pills feature tinted fills with solid borders: Green tint for "Hiring / Placement", Amber tint for "Cohort Filling Fast", and Cyan tint for "New Bootcamps".
- **Cards**:
  - Built with a pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `2px` border in `#0F172A`, and large `2rem` rounded corners.
  - Features internal padding using `space-lg`, pill tags anchored to top edges, and explicit action buttons aligned at the card base.
- **Input Fields**:
  - Pill-shaped field containers with `2px` borders.
  - Idle states use a slate border with subtle background tinting; focus states trigger a sharp cyan outer ring and hard 2px offset shadow without fuzzy focus rings.
- **Lists & Timeline Milestones**:
  - Career tracks and curriculum outlines are presented as modular pill blocks connected by a vertical `2px` dark slate line.
  - Completed modules are highlighted with emerald check indicators and high-contrast numerical badges.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Custom square-rounded (`checkbox`) and circular (`radio`) elements with `2px` slate outlines and bold check/dot states in `#06B6D4`.
